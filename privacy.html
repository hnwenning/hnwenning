Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

1. Introduction and Applicability

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") applies to all applications, websites, and services provided by Wenning Technology (Henan) Co., Ltd ("Company", "We", "Us", "Our"). This Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and otherwise process personal information when you use our applications, websites, and services, including those distributed through Google Play Store, Apple App Store, and other application marketplaces.

By accessing or using our applications and services, you consent to the practices described in this Policy. If you do not agree with our practices, please do not use our services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, postal address, date of birth, account credentials
  • Transaction Information: Payment information, billing address, transaction history, order details
  • Communication Information: Messages, feedback, support requests, survey responses
  • Profile Information: Profile picture, preferences, interests, demographic information

2.2 Information Automatically Collected

  • Device Information: Device type, operating system, device identifiers (IDFA, Android Advertising ID)
  • Usage Information: Features used, pages/screens viewed, time spent, navigation paths, crash logs
  • Location Information: IP address, GPS location (with permission), approximate location based on IP
  • Cookies and Similar Technologies: Cookies, pixels, web beacons, local storage

2.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Analytics providers
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media platforms
  • Data brokers
  • Public records

  • Demographic information (age range, gender)

4. Use of Information

We use collected information for:

  • Providing and improving our services
  • Processing transactions and sending related communications
  • Delivering personalized advertising and marketing
  • Analytics and measuring service performance
  • Customer support and technical assistance
  • Complying with legal obligations
  • Preventing fraud and ensuring security

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

5.1 Age Restrictions

  • General Users: 13 years and older can use our services independently
  • Under 18: Users under 18 require parental or guardian consent
  • Under 13 (COPPA): Users under 13 require explicit parental or guardian consent before using our services

5.2 COPPA Compliance (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act - USA)

If a child under 13 is identified:

  • We will not collect personal information beyond what is necessary for the service
  • We do not use behavioral advertising for users under 13
  • We provide parental access and control tools
  • We will not share children's data with third parties for marketing purposes

5.3 Parental Controls

Parents can:

  • Request access to children's personal information
  • Request deletion of children's data
  • Disable advertising and data collection
  • Manage consent preferences through the account settings

6. Data Retention

  • Account Information: Retained while account is active, deleted upon account termination
  • Transaction Information: Retained for 7 years for tax and legal compliance
  • Usage Data: Retained for 12 months for analytics purposes
  • Device Identifiers: Retained as set by advertising platforms
  • Location Data: Retained for 90 days unless extended for legal purposes

7. Application Store Compliance

7.1 Google Play Store Requirements

  • Compliance with Google Play Developer Program Policies
  • Clear privacy policy disclosure in app listing
  • Permission handling according to Android guidelines
  • Data safety declarations submitted and maintained
  • Restriction of data collection for children's apps

7.2 Apple App Store Requirements

  • Compliance with Apple App Store Review Guidelines
  • App Privacy Report disclosures
  • Privacy Labels for all tracked data
  • Transparency regarding third-party SDKs
  • Special requirements for apps targeting children (COPPA compliance)

8. Data Security

We implement security measures including:

  • End-to-end encryption for sensitive data
  • HTTPS protocol for all web communications
  • Secure authentication mechanisms
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Employee data protection training
  • Incident response procedures

9. Regional Privacy Laws and Compliance

9.1 European Union - GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)

  • Legal Basis: Consent, contract, legal obligation, vital interests
  • User Rights: Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection
  • Data Processing: By appointment of data processors only
  • DPA: Data Processing Addendum available upon request
  • Lawful Basis for Ads: Legitimate interest with proper consent

9.2 United States - CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)

  • Consumer Rights: Know, delete, opt-out, non-discrimination
  • Disclosure: Categories of personal information collected and shared
  • Do Not Sell Option: Conspicuous "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" link
  • Opt-Out Rights: Ability to prevent sale/sharing of data

9.3 United States - COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act)

  • Applies to services directed to children under 13
  • Parental consent requirement before collecting information
  • Restrictions on behavioral advertising for children
  • FTC enforcement and annual certifications

9.4 United States - VCDPA (Virginia Consumer Data Protection Act)

  • Consumer rights to access, correct, delete data
  • Opt-out from targeted advertising and sales
  • Right to opt-out of automated decision-making
  • Applies to businesses serving Virginia residents

9.5 Brazil - LGPD (Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados)

  • Personal data protection for Brazilian residents
  • Legal basis for processing required
  • Data subject rights: access, correction, deletion
  • Data Protection Officer appointment required
  • Compliance with LGPD Article 8 for children (under 18)

9.6 United Kingdom - UK GDPR

  • Similar to EU GDPR with UK-specific modifications
  • ICO (Information Commissioner's Office) enforcement
  • Post-Brexit adequacy decisions and data transfers

9.7 Canada - PIPEDA (Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act)

  • Privacy principles for Canadian residents
  • Consent requirement for information collection
  • Access and correction rights
  • Privacy complaint procedures

9.8 Japan - APPI (Act on Protection of Personal Information)

  • Personal information protection law for Japan
  • Purposes for use notification required
  • Cross-border transfer restrictions
  • Individual rights: access, correction, deletion

9.9 South Korea - PIPA (Personal Information Protection Act)

  • Comprehensive data protection framework
  • Consent requirements for collection and use
  • Data subject rights and remedies
  • Privacy impact assessment requirements

9.10 Thailand - PDPA (Personal Data Protection Act)

  • Privacy protection for Thai residents
  • Consent-based data collection
  • Personal data processor requirements
  • Compliance with Personal Data Protection Commission
