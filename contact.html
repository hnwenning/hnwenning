Frequently Asked Questions

What services does Wenning Technology provide?

We provide comprehensive enterprise technology solutions including software development, mobile app management, e-commerce platforms, digital marketing services, business consulting, and community services.

How do I get started with your services?

Simply contact us through our website contact form or reach out directly to our business support team at support@hnwenning.com. We'll schedule a consultation to discuss your specific needs and requirements.

What is your support availability?

We provide 24/7 technical support for our enterprise clients. Our support team is available via email, phone, and our online support portal to address any issues promptly.

Do you offer customized solutions?

Yes, we specialize in customized enterprise solutions. Our team works closely with clients to understand their unique requirements and develop tailored solutions that meet their specific business objectives.

What is your pricing model?

Our pricing is flexible and depends on the scope and complexity of your project. We offer various engagement models including fixed-price projects, time-and-materials, and ongoing support contracts.

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary based on complexity and scope. We provide detailed project planning and timelines during our initial consultation. Most enterprise projects range from 3-12 months depending on requirements.
